# New Crypto Tools 🚀

A few more useful resources to explore in 2025:

- **DeFiLlama** – Comprehensive dashboard for DeFi protocols, TVL rankings, and analytics.  
- **Dune Analytics** – Powerful platform for community-driven blockchain analytics.  
- **Glassnode** – On-chain metrics and insights for Bitcoin, Ethereum, and more.  
- **GMX Stats** – Real-time decentralized perpetual exchange analytics.  
- **DexScreener** – Live charts and data for tokens across multiple DEXes.
